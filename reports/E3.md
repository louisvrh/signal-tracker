# E3: the system's probabilities against prediction-market prices

*Checked 2026-10-07 (S25). Research only, not financial advice.*

## Headline

**Not testable on history.** No prediction market priced the official prints this project forecasts during the
test period (before the vault start, 2025-10-08). The few matching markets that exist opened in 2026, sit inside
the vault period, and have traded nothing. E3 can only be run forward.

## What was checked

**Kalshi** (docs: https://docs.kalshi.com; public market data needs no key; base URL
`https://external-api.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2`; `GET /series?category=Economics`, `GET /markets?series_ticker=...`,
settled markets under `GET /historical/markets`, prices under `GET /historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks`; the
historical cutoff on 2026-10-07 was 2026-08-08). Of 880 economics series, the ones matching E2's targets:

| Series | Title | Markets (settled + open) | Close dates | Traded volume (settled) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KXCHEXPORTS | China Exports YoY (monthly) | 15 + 0 | 2026-05-09 | 0 |
| KXCHIMPORTS | China Imports YoY (monthly) | 15 + 0 | 2026-05-09 | 0 |
| KXCHIPYOY | China Industrial Production YoY (monthly) | 15 + 0 | 2026-05-18 | 0 |
| KXSKEXPYOY | South Korea exports YoY (monthly) | 27 + 36 | 2026-05-31 to 2026-10-31 | 0 |
| KXUSIPMOM | US industrial production MoM (monthly) | 0 + 0 | – | – |

Kalshi also lists Japan, China and South Africa balance-of-trade series and many US-only series (ISM PMI, GDP,
state-level manufacturing employment); none overlaps E2's prints before 2026.

**Polymarket** (docs: https://docs.polymarket.com; `GET https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/public-search?q=...`, no
key): searches for "industrial production", "China exports", "exports", "manufacturing PMI" returned no market on
an official production or trade print (only company earnings and unrelated markets).

## How to run it forward

1. Kalshi's monthly markets are thresholds ("South Korea exports YoY above X%?"), while E2 forecasts "weaker than
   last month". Comparing needs P(YoY > X): the H1b-style nowcast (a point forecast with its out-of-sample error)
   gives that, assuming normal errors.
2. Each month, before a South Korea or China print, write the system's probability and Kalshi's last price for the
   same threshold as a pair of entries in `docs/JOURNAL.md`; `python -m tracker journal-score` then scores both.
3. Only markets with real volume are worth comparing; on 2026-10-07 the settled ones had none.
