## Method

- D_off end values from weekly snapshots (Mondays), the latest one in the 14 days before each monthly test date. The official series is the one that D_off value used: `oecd_ip` (154), `eurostat_ip` (126), `comtrade_exports` (79), `imf_exports` (74), `fred_indpro` (20), `comtrade_mirror_exports` (19), `oecd_ip_manufacturing` (19).
- Event: the YoY of the first month not yet published is lower than the YoY of the last published month. Each (country, month) is forecast once, on the first test date it is the next print.
- Logistic regression of the event on D_off, pooled over countries, refitted on each date on events already resolved then (labels as known then), needing 60; the base rate is the share of those events that happened. Scored on outcomes as known when the vault starts.
- Variants tried: this one only.

## Caveats

- D_off exists only from January 2024 (its activity z-score needs two years of composite scores), so the sample is about a year and a half and the model's first fits use few events.
- Most history has estimated publication dates (PLAN §5.1); whether a print was out on a test date is only approximately right for backfilled data.
